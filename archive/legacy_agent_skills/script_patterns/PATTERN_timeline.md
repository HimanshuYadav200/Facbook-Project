# Script Pattern: The Timeline

**Inherits**: BASE_VIRAL_STRUCTURE.md

**Pattern Type**: Multi-Decision Journey → Compounding Results → Final Outcome

**Emotional Arc**: Anticipation → Progress → Setbacks → Resolution

---

## Pattern Overview

Track Skulsy through 3-5 connected decisions over 5-20 years. Each decision affects the next. Cumulative outcome revealed at end.

**Core Question**: "Where did all these decisions lead?"

**Viewer Psychology**: Long-term thinking + consequence chains + "life is a series of choices"

---

## Pattern Structure (45 seconds)

### Beat 1: The Starting Point (0-8s)

**Hook Formula** (Journey Setup):
"Skulsy made [X] decisions over [Y] years. Here's what happened."

**Examples**:
- "Skulsy built a real estate portfolio. 5 properties. 10 years."
- "Skulsy tried 4 different investment strategies. Here's how each went."
- "Skulsy's retirement plan. 20 years. 6 decisions."

**State**: `"hook"`  
**Intensity**: 0.5  
**Timeline Anchor**: Start year

---

### Beat 2: Decision 1 (8-16s)

**Goal**: First choice + outcome

**Pattern**: Decision → Result → Lesson

**Example**:
```
"2010. Bought first rental. $150k.
Rented for $1,200/month.
Mortgage: $1,000.
Positive cash flow. Good start."
```

**State**: `"optimism"`  
**Intensity**: 0.6  
**Timeline Anchor**: Decision 1 year  
**Retention Trigger**: "stakes"

---

### Beat 3: Decision 2 (16-24s)

**Goal**: Second choice building on first

**Pattern**: Compound effect

**Example**:
```
"2013. Used equity. Bought second property.
$200k. Rented for $1,500.
Two properties. $500/month total cash flow."
```

**State**: `"growth"`  
**Intensity**: 0.7  
**Timeline Anchor**: Decision 2 year

---

### Beat 4: Decision 3 + Complication (24-32s)

**Goal**: Stakes raised, problem emerges

**Pattern**: Success → Challenge

**Example**:
```
"2016. Third property. $250k.
But. Interest rates rose.
Rents stagnated.
Cash flow: $300/month. Shrinking."
```

**State**: `"doubt"` or `"tension"`  
**Intensity**: 0.75  
**Timeline Anchor**: Decision 3 year  
**Retention Trigger**: "pattern_break"

---

### Beat 5: Resolution (32-45s)

**Goal**: Final outcome of the journey

**Punchline Types**:

**Option A - Success**:
"2025. Owns 5 properties. Worth $1.2M. Paid $600k. Net: $600k."

**Option B - Mixed**:
"Worth $1.2M on paper. Owes $800k. Net: $400k. Not as good as stocks."

**Option C - Failure**:
"2020. Pandemic hit. Couldn't evict. Foreclosure. Lost everything."

**State**: `"punchline"`  
**Intensity**: 0.85-0.95  
**Retention Trigger**: "callback"

---

## Pattern-Specific Mechanics

### The Compounding Narrative

Each decision builds on the last.

**Pattern**:
Decision 1 → enables → Decision 2 → enables → Decision 3

Not:
Decision 1. Decision 2. Decision 3. (unconnected)

### The Momentum Shift

Around decision 3-4, introduce a complication.

**Why**: Keeps viewer engaged, adds drama

**Examples**:
- Market crash
- Interest rate change
- Personal circumstance
- Competition increases

### The Time Collapse

Compress years without losing narrative.

**Technique**: "By [YEAR]..." phrases

**Example**:
"By 2015, three properties. By 2020, five. By 2025, broke."

---

## Visual Direction

- Beat 1: Skulsy at starting point
- Beat 2-4: Properties/assets accumulating (or depleting)
- Timeline graphic showing progression
- Beat 5: Final state comparison (then vs now)

---

## Research Requirements

- Multiple decision points (3-5)
- Outcomes for each decision
- Timeline (years between decisions)
- Cumulative final result
- Market context at each point

---

## Quality Metrics

- **Connection**: Does each decision logically follow? (85+)
- **Progression**: Do stakes build? (80+)
- **Pacing**: Balanced across timeline? (75+)

---

## Example Timelines

**Good Fits**:
- Real estate portfolio building
- Career progression + investments
- Retirement account decisions
- Business growth stages
- Debt payoff journey

**Poor Fits**:
- Single decision with many outcomes
- Unconnected decisions
- Too many decisions (>5)
