# Script Pattern: The Success

**Inherits**: BASE_VIRAL_STRUCTURE.md

**Pattern Type**: Contrarian Decision → Vindication → Surprise Twist

**Emotional Arc**: Doubt → Vindication → (Optional) Ironic Reversal

---

## Pattern Overview

Skulsy makes a GOOD decision (for once). Everyone doubts him. He's vindicated. Optional twist: he ruins it afterward.

**Core Question**: "Did Skulsy actually get it right?"

**Viewer Psychology**: Surprise (Skulsy winning?!) + vicarious pride + expectation of twist

---

## Pattern Structure (45 seconds)

### Beat 1: The Contrarian Setup (0-8s)

**Hook Formula** (Unexpected Contrast):
"Skulsy [GOOD DECISION] when everyone said [OPPOSITE]"

**Examples**:
- "Skulsy bought when everyone was selling"
- "Skulsy held through the crash. Everyone called him crazy."
- "Skulsy ignored the experts. He was right."

**State**: `"hook"`  
**Intensity**: 0.5  
**Timeline Anchor**: Decision year

---

### Beat 2: The Doubt (8-20s)

**Goal**: Show why everyone thought he was wrong

**Pattern**: Stack the evidence against him

**Example**:
```
"2008. Financial crisis. Markets down 50%.
Every expert: 'Sell everything.'
His friends: 'You're insane.'
He held."
```

**State**: `"doubt"` or `"tension"`  
**Intensity**: 0.6-0.7  
**Retention Trigger**: "contrast"

---

### Beat 3: The Vindication (20-35s)

**Goal**: Show him being proven right

**Pattern**: Progressive wins

**Example**:
```
"2009. Markets up 30%.
2010. Up another 15%.
By 2012, he'd recovered everything.
By 2015, he'd doubled his money."
```

**State**: `"optimism"` or `"growth"`  
**Intensity**: 0.7-0.8  
**Timeline Anchor**: Recovery milestones  
**Retention Trigger**: "surprise" (he's actually winning)

---

### Beat 4: The Resolution (35-45s)

**Punchline Options**:

**Option A - Pure Win** (rare):
"He made $500k. Retired at 45. Never touched it again."

**Option B - The Twist** (common):
"Then he got confident. Bet it all on crypto. Lost everything."

**Option C - The Lesson Ignored**:
"He proved everyone wrong. Then tried to do it again. Didn't work twice."

**State**: `"punchline"`  
**Intensity**: 0.8-0.9  
**Retention Trigger**: "callback" or "twist"

---

## Pattern-Specific Mechanics

### The Underdog Arc

Viewer roots for Skulsy because everyone else is against him.

**Technique**: Emphasize the skepticism

"His broker: 'This is financial suicide.'  
His wife: 'We're going to lose everything.'  
His dad: 'I didn't raise you to be this stupid.'"

### The Earned Win

Show WHY it worked (luck vs. insight).

**Good** ✅:
"He bought real assets. Low prices. Simple math."

**Avoid** ❌:
"He just got lucky."

---

## Visual Direction

- Beat 1: Skulsy alone against crowd
- Beat 2: Everyone pointing/mocking
- Beat 3: Chart recovering, Skulsy calm
- Beat 4: Skulsy vindicated (or twist reveal)

---

## Research Requirements

- Entry point (when he bought)
- Prevailing sentiment (what experts said)
- Recovery timeline
- Final outcome

---

## Quality Metrics

- **Contrast**: Is the skepticism clear? (80+)
- **Vindication**: Is the win satisfying? (80+)
- **Authenticity**: Does success feel earned? (70+)
