# Script Pattern: The Mistake

**Inherits**: BASE_VIRAL_STRUCTURE.md

**Pattern Type**: Bad Decision → Accumulating Consequences → Impact

**Emotional Arc**: Setup → Tension → Escalation → Regret

---

## Pattern Overview

Skulsy makes a specific, identifiable financial mistake. We watch it unfold with deadpan narration, letting the bad decision speak for itself.

**Core Question**: "How bad did this get?"

**Viewer Psychology**: Schadenfreude + recognition ("I almost did this") + relief ("at least I didn't...")

---

## Pattern Structure (45 seconds)

### Beat 1: The Setup (0-8s)

**Hook Formula** (Relatable Mistake):
"Skulsy [MISTAKE] at the [WORST/BEST] possible moment"

**Examples**:
- "Skulsy panic-sold at the bottom"
- "Skulsy bought GameStop at $483"
- "Skulsy used his rent money for options"

**Requirements**:
- Specific mistake (not vague)
- Timing context (when it was worst)
- Relatable error (viewers recognize)

**State**: `"hook"` + `"setup"`  
**Intensity**: 0.5-0.6  
**Timeline Anchor**: Year/month of mistake

---

### Beat 2: The Mistake Details (8-20s)

**Goal**: Show exactly what he did wrong

**Pattern**: Break down the bad decision

**Structure**:
1. Context: "The market was..."
2. His action: "He decided to..."
3. Red flags ignored: "No research. No plan. Pure emotion."

**Example**:
```
"March 2020. Markets crashed 30%.
He sold everything at the bottom.
S&P 500 at 2,200.
No plan. Pure panic."
```

**State**: `"doubt"` or `"panic"`  
**Intensity**: 0.6-0.7  
**Retention Trigger**: "setup" (building dread)

---

### Beat 3: The Unraveling (20-35s)

**Goal**: Show consequences accumulating

**Pattern**: "First X. Then Y. Then Z."

**Structure**:
1. Immediate consequence
2. Bigger consequence
3. Final blow

**Example**:
```
"Two weeks later, markets recovered.
One month later, new all-time highs.
His portfolio would've doubled.
He locked in a 30% loss."
```

**State**: `"regret"` or `"consequence"`  
**Intensity**: 0.8-0.9 (climbing)  
**Timeline Anchor**: Aftermath dates  
**Retention Trigger**: "stakes" (escalating)

---

### Beat 4: The Aftermath (35-45s)

**Punchline Types**:

**Option A - The Permanent Impact**:
"He lost $50,000. Permanently. It's gone."

**Option B - The Comparison**:
"If he'd done nothing, he'd have $200k. He has $140k."

**Option C - The Irony**:
"He sold to avoid risk. Took the biggest risk possible."

**Option D - The Lesson Refused**:
"He learned his lesson. Bought crypto at the peak. Again."

**State**: `"punchline"`  
**Intensity**: 0.85-0.95  
**Retention Trigger**: "callback"

---

## Pattern-Specific Mechanics

### The Avoidability Factor

Make it clear this was PREVENTABLE.

**Technique**: Show the red flags

**Example**:
"The PE ratio was 500. Every analyst said sell. He bought more."

### The Escalation

Each consequence should be worse than the last.

**Bad** ❌:
"He lost $1k. Then $2k. Then $3k."

**Good** ✅:
"He lost $1k. Then his gains. Then his principal. Then borrowed money."

---

## Visual Direction

- Beat 1: Skulsy + decision moment
- Beat 2: Red charts, warning signs
- Beat 3: Cascading losses
- Beat 4: Empty portfolio, Skulsy static

---

## Research Requirements

- Exact entry price/date
- Market context (what was happening)
- Outcome price/date
- Opportunity cost (what could've been)

---

## Quality Metrics

- **Relatability**: Is this a common mistake? (80+)
- **Escalation**: Do consequences build? (80+)
- **Specificity**: Exact numbers? (100)
