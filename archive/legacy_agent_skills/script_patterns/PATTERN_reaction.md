# Script Pattern: The Reaction

**Inherits**: BASE_VIRAL_STRUCTURE.md

**Pattern Type**: Event Announcement → Skulsy's Take → Historical Context → Implications

**Emotional Arc**: Alert → Analysis → Pattern Recognition → Projection

---

## Pattern Overview

Breaking news or market event happens. Skulsy reacts with his characteristic deadpan analysis, often drawing parallels to his past mistakes.

**Core Question**: "What does this actually mean?"

**Viewer Psychology**: Currency + clarity + "someone explain this to me" + dark humor

---

## Pattern Structure (45 seconds)

### Beat 1: The Event (0-8s)

**Hook Formula** (News Breaking):
"[EVENT] just happened. Here's what Skulsy thinks."

**Examples**:
- "The Fed just raised rates 0.75%. Here's what that means."
- "Silicon Valley Bank collapsed. Skulsy's seen this before."
- "Bitcoin hit $100k. Skulsy has thoughts."

**State**: `"hook"`  
**Intensity**: 0.6  
**Timeline Anchor**: Today/This week

---

### Beat 2: The Immediate Impact (8-20s)

**Goal**: What actually happened (facts)

**Pattern**: Break down the event

**Example (SVB Collapse)**:
```
"Silicon Valley Bank. $200B in assets.
Failed in 48 hours.
Tech startups couldn't access cash.
Payroll frozen.
Second largest bank failure in US history."
```

**State**: `"analysis"`  
**Intensity**: 0.7  
**Retention Trigger**: "stakes"

---

### Beat 3: The Pattern (20-32s)

**Goal**: Historical parallel (this happened before)

**Pattern**: "Last time..."

**Example**:
```
"Last time a bank failed this fast? 2008.
Washington Mutual. $300B.
Before that? Continental Illinois. 1984.
Pattern: Rapid withdrawals. Liquidity crisis. Contagion risk."
```

**State**: `"insight"`  
**Intensity**: 0.75  
**Timeline Anchor**: Historical comparison  
**Retention Trigger**: "pattern_recognition"

---

### Beat 4: Skulsy's Take (32-45s)

**Goal**: The deadpan insight/prediction

**Punchline Types**:

**Option A - The Irony**:
"Skulsy had $500 in SVB. Lost access. Learned about FDIC insurance the hard way."

**Option B - The Pattern Callback**:
"He's seen this before. 2008. He panic-sold then too. Probably will again."

**Option C - The Projection**:
"More banks will fail. Or the Fed will bail them out. Either way, inflation."

**Option D - The Detachment**:
"Skulsy doesn't bank with SVB. Or Chase. Or anyone. Cash under mattress. Different problem."

**State**: `"punchline"`  
**Intensity**: 0.8-0.9  
**Retention Trigger**: "callback" or "twist"

---

## Pattern-Specific Mechanics

### The Timeliness

This pattern REQUIRES recent news (within 7 days).

**Rule**: If event is older than 1 week, use a different pattern.

### The Clarity Through Deadpan

Complex events explained simply, no panic.

❌ BAD:
"This is terrifying! Everyone should be worried!"

✅ GOOD:
"Three banks failed. More will follow. Or they won't. History suggests they will."

### The Personal Connection

Link the macro event to Skulsy's micro experience.

**Pattern**:
"[BIG EVENT] happened.  
Skulsy [SMALL RELATED THING].  
He understands now."

---

## Voice Guidelines

### Explain Simply, Not Sensationally

This is NEWS, not commentary.

❌ BAD:
"The END of banking as we know it!"

✅ GOOD:
"A bank failed. Here's what that means for deposits."

### No Predictions (Unless Wrong)

Skulsy can predict, but only if it's likely wrong or deadpan.

**Acceptable**:
"He predicts: More failures. Or not. He's usually wrong."

**Not Acceptable**:
"This means you should [ADVICE]."

---

## Visual Direction

- Beat 1: News headline/breaking news graphic
- Beat 2: Data visualization (numbers, charts)
- Beat 3: Historical footage/comparison
- Beat 4: Skulsy reacting (or not reacting)

---

## Research Requirements

- Event facts (what happened, when)
- Numbers (how big, how many affected)
- Historical precedent (last time this happened)
- Current status (ongoing or resolved)

---

## Quality Metrics

- **Accuracy**: Is the event explained correctly? (100)
- **Clarity**: Can viewer understand it? (85+)
- **Relevance**: Is this newsworthy enough? (80+)

---

## Example Events for This Pattern

**Good Fits**:
- Fed rate decisions
- Bank failures
- Major market crashes/rallies
- Regulation changes
- Crypto events
- Real estate market shifts
- Corporate bankruptcies

**Poor Fits**:
- Individual stock moves (unless massive)
- Personal finance tips
- Opinion pieces
- Old news (>1 week)

---

## Variation: The Prediction Review

Look back at what Skulsy predicted vs what happened.

**Hook**: "One year ago, Skulsy predicted [X]. Here's what actually happened."

**Structure**:
1. His prediction
2. What actually happened
3. How wrong he was
4. Why he was wrong
