# Script Pattern: The Comparison

**Inherits**: BASE_VIRAL_STRUCTURE.md

**Pattern Type**: Option A vs Option B → Analysis → Verdict

**Emotional Arc**: Curiosity → Analysis → Surprise → Resolution

---

## Pattern Overview

Skulsy faces two choices. We watch both paths unfold in parallel, then reveal which was better (often with a twist).

**Core Question**: "Which choice was right?"

**Viewer Psychology**: Decision paralysis recognition + hindsight clarity + "I'm facing this choice now"

---

## Pattern Structure (45 seconds)

### Beat 1: The Fork (0-8s)

**Hook Formula** (Decision Point):
"Skulsy had [AMOUNT]. Two options: [A] or [B]."

**Examples**:
- "Skulsy had $100k. Buy a house or invest in stocks?"
- "$10k. Bitcoin or gold?"
- "2001. Amazon or Cisco?"

**State**: `"hook"`  
**Intensity**: 0.5  
**Timeline Anchor**: Decision year

---

### Beat 2: Path A (8-20s)

**Goal**: Show outcome of first choice

**Pattern**: Track performance over time

**Example (House)**:
```
"The house: $300k in 2010.
By 2015: $350k.
By 2020: $450k.
Total gain: $150k.
Plus equity. Minus mortgage interest."
```

**State**: `"analysis"`  
**Intensity**: 0.6  
**Timeline Anchor**: Milestones for A  
**Retention Trigger**: "stakes"

---

### Beat 3: Path B (20-32s)

**Goal**: Show outcome of second choice

**Pattern**: Same timeline, different numbers

**Example (Stocks)**:
```
"The stocks: $100k in S&P 500.
By 2015: $180k.
By 2020: $320k.
Total gain: $220k.
No equity. But liquid."
```

**State**: `"analysis"`  
**Intensity**: 0.7  
**Timeline Anchor**: Same milestones for B  
**Retention Trigger**: "contrast"

---

### Beat 4: The Verdict (32-45s)

**Punchline Types**:

**Option A - Clear Winner**:
"Stocks won. By $70k. But he bought the house."

**Option B - Unexpected Tie**:
"Almost identical. House: $150k. Stocks: $160k. The 'right' choice didn't matter."

**Option C - Hidden Costs**:
"Stocks won on paper. But he panic-sold in 2015. Lost to house."

**Option D - Neither Won**:
"He chose crypto. Lost everything. Should've picked either."

**State**: `"punchline"`  
**Intensity**: 0.85-0.9  
**Retention Trigger**: "callback" + "surprise"

---

## Pattern-Specific Mechanics

### The Parallel Track

Keep timeline aligned for fair comparison.

**Structure**:
- Year X: Option A did [Y], Option B did [Z]
- Year X+5: Option A at [Y2], Option B at [Z2]

### The Hidden Variable

Reveal a factor that changes the analysis.

**Examples**:
- Taxes
- Inflation
- Opportunity cost
- Emotional toll

**Pattern**:
"On paper, stocks won.  
But property gave equity.  
And he couldn't panic-sell a house."

---

## Visual Direction

- Beat 1: Skulsy at crossroads, two paths
- Beat 2: Track A (one side of screen)
- Beat 3: Track B (other side, split screen)
- Beat 4: Paths converge, verdict revealed

---

## Research Requirements

- Option A performance (with timeline)
- Option B performance (same timeline)
- Hidden costs (taxes, fees, etc.)
- Final net outcome for both

---

## Quality Metrics

- **Fairness**: Same timeline for both? (100)
- **Clarity**: Can viewer compare easily? (85+)
- **Surprise**: Is verdict unexpected? (70+)

---

## Example Comparisons

**Good Fits**:
- Stocks vs Real Estate
- Bitcoin vs Gold
- Renting vs Buying
- Index funds vs Active management
- Pay off debt vs Invest
- Company A vs Company B (same sector)

**Poor Fits**:
- Unrelated assets (Bitcoin vs Real Estate)
- Different time frames
- Incomparable options
