January, twenty sixteen. Skulsy put one thousand dollars into a company that made graphics cards for video games. Nvidia. His friends laughed. Toys for gamers, they said. By the end of twenty twenty, his thousand dollars had grown to eighteen thousand. A year later? Forty five thousand. Then twenty twenty two hit. The stock lost more than half its value. Everyone sold. Skulsy didn't. Then the world discovered A I, and every A I needed Nvidia's chips. Today, that one thousand dollars is worth about two hundred and eighty seven thousand. He wasn't smarter than anyone. He just sat still.
