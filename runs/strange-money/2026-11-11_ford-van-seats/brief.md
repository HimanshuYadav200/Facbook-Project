# Brief: ford-van-seats

Ford imported Turkish-built Transit Connect vans as passenger wagons (seats, belts, rear windows) to pay 2.5% instead of the 25% "chicken tax", then stripped them into cargo vans in Baltimore. Customs ruled against it (2013), Supreme Court declined Ford's appeal (2020), Ford settled for $365M (2024) without admitting liability. Told as a true story, not a how-to.
