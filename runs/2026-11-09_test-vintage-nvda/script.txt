January 2016. Skulsy put $1,000 into a company that made graphics cards for video games. Nvidia. His friends laughed. Toys for gamers, they said. By the end of 2020, his $1,000 had grown to $18,200. A year later? $41,100. Then 2022 hit. The stock lost more than half its value. Everyone sold. Skulsy didn't. Then the world discovered A I, and every A I needed Nvidia's chips. Today, that $1,000 is worth about $262,000. He wasn't smarter than anyone. He just sat still.
