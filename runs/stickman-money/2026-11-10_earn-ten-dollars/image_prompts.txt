# STYLE (prepend to every prompt): Black and white pencil sketch illustration, vertical 9:16. Simple stickman characters with big round blank white heads, two small dot eyes, a small mouth, no nose, no hair (women have long hair), smooth white bodies with soft grey shading. Clean, detailed hand-drawn background in light grey. Soft vignette. No text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere. Characters in the lower two thirds, top of the picture kept simple.
