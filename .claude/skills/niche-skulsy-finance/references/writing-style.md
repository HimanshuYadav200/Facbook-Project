# Writing style (sound human, stay honest)

Adapted from the legacy humanizer skill (`archive/legacy_agent_skills/humanizer/SKILL.md`, based on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing"). Changed: the old "opinionated stance" advice (ban "it depends", write "This proves…") is **dropped for finance**: overclaiming certainty about money is exactly what we must not do.

## Do
- Short sentences, with the odd longer one. Fragments are fine. For effect.
- Plain words: "use" not "leverage", "look at" not "delve into".
- Concrete over abstract: "$1,000 in January 2016", not "a modest investment years ago".
- Deadpan humour in the understatement: "He wasn't smarter than anyone. He just sat still."
- Repeat a key word instead of swapping synonyms (the viewer should hear "Nvidia" every time, not "the chipmaker", "the company", "the firm").
- Read it aloud: would you say it to a friend?

## Don't
- AI vocabulary: delve, leverage, tapestry, landscape, realm, testament, pivotal, robust, seamless, transformative, game-changer, navigate, foster, harness, elevate, unleash.
- Filler openers / connectors: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, It is important to note, In conclusion.
- "Not only… but also", "It's not just X, it's Y", groups of three adjectives.
- Inflated significance: "a pivotal moment", "left an indelible mark".
- Vague sources: "experts say", "studies show". Name it or cut it.
- Preaching at the end ("So remember, kids…"). One plain line is enough.
- Certainty about the future: "will", "always", "guaranteed". Past tense for what happened; "can"/"often" for patterns.
